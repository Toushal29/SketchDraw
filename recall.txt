New project and planning screens
- Reusable project templates: start from a project plan, product roadmap, event plan, study plan, or meeting workspace.
Let users switch between board, timeline, list, and calendar views of the same tasks. That makes planning feel like one coherent feature rather than a collection of separate tools.



Features beyond planning
- Notes and lightweight documents: write meeting notes, briefs, specs, and research notes linked to tasks or sketches.
- Knowledge base: connect notes and projects with backlinks, tags, and full-text search.
- Meeting mode: prepare an agenda, keep notes, track decisions, and capture follow-up tasks.
- Review mode: add comments and annotations to project items, then resolve them.
- Activity and restore history: see what changed and recover an earlier project state, useful when a shared folder syncs edits across devices.
- Interactive presentations: add speaker notes and link slides or canvas areas into a guided walkthrough.
- Focus tools: a task timer, daily priorities, and a “next action” view.
